MyLab Math (MLM) Grade Example

MLM grade calculation is based on the Weighted Average method.
Each section has equal weights.
This is how your MLM grades are calculated.

(1.) The MyLab Math (MLM) has 4 chapters: Chapters 2, 3, 4, 5.

(2.) The 4 chapters has 25 main sections.
These are Sections: 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4, 2.5, 2.6, 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4, 3.5, 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 4.4, 4.5, 4.6, 4.7, 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 5.4, 5.5, 5.6, 11.2
Because I do not want to overwhelm you with many questions per section, each section is further divided into more sections.
Hence, the 25 sections are further divided into 71 sections.
So, we are dealing with 71 sections. Please see them in the calculator.

(3.) 71 sections count for 100% on MLM
This implies that each section count for

$ \dfrac{100}{71} = 1.408450704\% \\[5ex] $ This is what should be entered in the Weight section of the calculator.

(4.) Let us do an example.
Assume Student A made a:
98% on Section 2.1-1st
90% on Section 2.1-2nd
86% on Section 2.1-3rd
Student A is aware that 100% can be made on all three sections by doing similar questions of the questions got incorrectly.
However, Student A is content with the grades made so far and wants to find the current grade.
What is the current grade for Student A?

Student A's current grade
MLM Section Weight (%) Your Score (%) Weighted Score
(Weight * Your Score)
Section 2.1-1st 1.408450704 98 138.028169
Section 2.1-2nd 1.408450704 90 126.7605634
Section 2.1-3rd 1.408450704 86 121.1267605
$\Sigma Weights = 4.225352112$ $\Sigma Weighted\;\;Scores = 385.9154929$
$ Current\;\;Grade = \dfrac{\Sigma Weighted\;\;Scores}{\Sigma Weights} = \dfrac{385.9154929}{4.225352112} = 91.33333333 \approx 91\% $

Student A's current MLM grade based on these three sections is 91%.
This is the grade that the student will see in the MLM gradebook.
You may verify your calculations with the MLM Grade Calculator.

Final Grade Example

Final grade calculation is also based on the Weighted Average method.
Howevever, each assessment does not have has equal weight.
This is how your final grade is calculated.

Final Grade Example
Assessments Weight (%) Your Score (%) Weighted Score
(Weight * Your Score)
MyLab Math 70 90 6300
Test 1 5 95 475
Test 2 5 85 425
Test 3 5 80 400
Project 10 100 1000
Final Exam 5 70 350
$\Sigma Weights = 100$ $\Sigma Weighted\;\;Scores = 8950$
$ Current\;\;Grade = \dfrac{\Sigma Weighted\;\;Scores}{\Sigma Weights} = \dfrac{8950}{100} = 89.5 \approx 90\% $

Please NOTE:
(1.) This is the grade you will see in the Canvas gradebook.

(2.) You must not complete all fields.
You can calculate your current grade at any time during the course based on the assessments you have done.
Then, you can verify your calculations with the calculator.

(3.) The final grade is rounded to the nearest integer only one time.
A grade of 89.50000001% is rounded to a 90% which is an A while a grade of 89.499999 is rounded to an 80% which is a B.

(4.) At least a final grade of 70% (C) is required to pass the course.

(5.) There is no extra credit or bonus point or curving grades for the course.
Please review the Frequently Asked Questions for explanations.

(6.) Please send an email to me and/or attend the Office Hours/Live Sessions if you have any questions.
